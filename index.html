Privacy Policy

Applies to: RFX Discord bot · RFX Discord server · RFX website

RFX ("we", "us", "our") operates a Discord bot (the "Bot"), a Discord community server (the "Server") and a website (the "Site"), together the "Services". By using any of the Services you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please stop using the Services and remove the Bot from your server.

We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we only collect what is needed for the Bot's features to work.

2.3 Purchase and payment data

Payments in RFX are made with ProBot credits inside Discord. We read the ProBot transfer confirmation message in the payment channel and store: the payer's Discord ID, the amount, the item purchased, the time and the related order/ticket number. We never receive or store bank cards, passwords, or any financial account details.

Purchased scripts may carry a unique license code / watermark tied to the buyer's Discord ID so that leaked copies can be traced.

2.4 Moderation data

When moderation actions are taken (warnings, timeouts, kicks, bans, blacklist), we store the target's Discord ID, the moderator's ID, the reason and the time. Anti-spam and anti-raid systems may temporarily process message content to detect spam, links or mass mentions; that content is not kept after the check.

2.5 Website data

The Site is hosted on Google Firebase. When you visit it, Firebase may sign you in with an anonymous identifier so that view, copy and download counters are counted once per visitor. We do not ask for your name or email on the public site. Admin accounts use a username and password that are managed by Firebase Authentication. Firebase (Google) may log technical data such as IP address and browser type for security and operation.

2.6 Data we do NOT collect

We do not read your direct messages with other people, we do not store message content outside ticket channels, we do not collect your email address, phone number, real name, location or payment card details through the Bot.

How We Use Data

  • To run the Bot's features: tickets, ratings, store and delivery, giveaways, events, levels, invites, Wheel of Fortune and server setup.
  • To deliver purchased or won items to you by direct message.
  • To keep the Server safe: verification, anti-spam, anti-raid and moderation.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, chargeback-style disputes and leaking of paid content.
  • To keep logs and transcripts for support history and staff quality review.
  • To make backups so data is not lost if the hosting fails.
  • To show public statistics on the Site (for example how many times a script was viewed or copied).

The legal bases we rely on are: performing the service you asked for, our legitimate interest in keeping the Services safe and working, and your consent where required.

Security

Bot tokens and API keys are kept in private environment files and are never published. The website database uses strict security rules so visitors can only read public content and update counters once, and only verified admins can change content. The public website code is minified and obfuscated. No system is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data and will act quickly on any incident.

Your Rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • Access — get a copy of the data we hold about your Discord ID.
  • Correct — fix data that is wrong.
  • Delete — erase your data (see Data Deletion).
  • Object / restrict — stop certain processing, for example invite tracking.
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, by leaving the Server or removing the Bot.

If you are in the EU/UK (GDPR), California (CCPA/CPRA) or Saudi Arabia (PDPL), you also have the rights given by those laws, including the right to complain to your local data protection authority. We answer requests within 30 days.

Children

Discord requires users to be at least 13 years old (or the minimum age in your country). The Services are not intended for anyone under that age, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 is using the Services, contact us and we will delete the data.

Cookies & Local Storage

The Site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It uses browser storage only for:

  • Firebase Authentication session (anonymous visitor ID or admin login).
  • Remembering simple preferences on your device.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address. You can clear site data from your browser at any time.

Data Deletion

To delete your data:

  1. Open a ticket in the RFX Discord server and write "Data deletion request", or contact the server owner directly.

Some records may be kept where we have a valid reason, such as purchase and license records needed to prevent leaks and fraud, or moderation/blacklist records needed for safety. Server owners can remove all data for their server by removing the Bot and asking us to wipe it.
